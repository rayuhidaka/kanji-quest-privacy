Who we are

Kanji Quest RPG ("the game") is made by the independent developer Ray Uwe Hidaka (Naomi.Jpn) ("we"). Contact: nomi.ai.jpn@gmail.com.

The short version

  • We run no servers and receive none of your data. Your progress stays on your device.
  • Your handwriting is read on your device and never leaves it.
  • The game shows ads from Google AdMob, which uses device data as described below. In the EEA, UK and Switzerland you choose first, and can change your choice in Settings → Privacy options.
  • Signing in to Google Play Games is optional.

Data stored on your device

The game saves profiles on your device: the nickname you type, your kanji collection and progress, coins, items, settings, quests and records. This data is not sent to us or anyone else. You can delete a profile in the game (profile screen → Delete) or delete everything by uninstalling the game or clearing its storage. Please don't use your real full name as a nickname.

Reminders (optional)

If you turn them on, the game can remind you when kanji in another grade are fading. The reminder is prepared on your device from your saved progress, and nothing is sent to us or anyone else. You can turn reminders off in the game's Settings or in your phone's settings.

Handwriting recognition (Google ML Kit)

Kanji you draw are recognized on the device by Google's ML Kit Digital Ink Recognition. Your drawings are not uploaded. The first time, the game downloads the Japanese recognition model (about 20 MB) from Google. Like other Google SDKs, ML Kit may contact Google to download or update models and may send Google technical information about how the feature performs (for example device type, app version and error counts), which does not include your drawings. See ML Kit Terms.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game shows a banner while you walk on the maps and in rooms, and optional rewarded videos (you choose to watch one, for example to double your coins or to revive). Ads are never shown during battles, drawing or the tutorial. To show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google's Mobile Ads SDK collects and processes data such as:

  • the device's advertising ID and other device identifiers,
  • IP address (used to estimate an approximate location),
  • device and app information (model, OS version, language, app version) and diagnostics,
  • interactions with ads (views, taps).

In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, the game asks for your consent through Google's consent form before ads are loaded, and you can change your choice any time in Settings → Privacy options. Elsewhere you can limit ad personalization or reset/delete your advertising ID in your Android settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads). Learn how Google uses this data: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Google Play Games (optional)

If you sign in to Google Play Games, Google provides the game with your Play Games player ID and display name, and the game sends your daily points and unlocked achievements to Google. Your display name and score can be seen by other players on the leaderboard. This is handled by Google under the Google Privacy Policy; you can manage your Play Games profile and visibility in the Google Play Games app. The game works fully without signing in.

Crash reports

If you have allowed it in your Android settings, Google Play may send us anonymous crash and performance reports (Android vitals). They do not identify you.

What we don't do

  • No account with us, no analytics of our own, no selling of data.
  • No access to your contacts, photos, location services, camera or microphone.
  • No in-app purchases at this time.

Children

The game is intended for players aged 13 and over who are learning Japanese. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Your rights

Because we hold no personal data, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete on our side; the data on your device is under your control as described above. For data processed by Google (ads, ML Kit, Play Games), you can use Google's controls at myaccount.google.com and in your Android settings.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date. Questions: nomi.ai.jpn@gmail.com.

手書き認識（Google ML Kit）

書いた漢字は Google の ML Kit Digital Ink Recognition により端末内で認識され、手書きデータはアップロードされません。初回のみ、日本語の認識モデル（約20MB）を Google からダウンロードします。ML Kit は、モデルのダウンロード・更新のために Google と通信し、機能の動作に関する技術情報（端末の種類、アプリのバージョン、エラーの件数など。手書きデータは含みません）を Google に送信する場合があります。ML Kit 利用規約をご覧ください。

広告（Google AdMob）

本ゲームは、マップや部屋を歩いているときにバナー広告を、また任意のリワード動画（コインを2倍にする、復活するなど、ご自身で選んだときのみ）を表示します。バトル中・手書き中・チュートリアル中には広告を表示しません。広告の配信・効果測定・不正防止のため、Google Mobile Ads SDK は次のような情報を収集・処理します：

  • 広告ID などの端末識別子
  • IPアドレス（おおよその地域の推定に使用）
  • 端末とアプリの情報（機種、OSのバージョン、言語、アプリのバージョン）と診断情報
  • 広告の表示・タップなどの操作

EEA・英国・スイスでは、広告を読み込む前に Google の同意フォームで同意をお聞きします。選択は「せってい → プライバシーの せってい（こうこく）」からいつでも変更できます。その他の地域では、Android の設定（設定 → プライバシー → 広告）で広告のカスタマイズを制限したり、広告ID をリセット・削除したりできます。Google によるデータの利用についてはGoogle のサービスを使用するサイトやアプリから収集した情報の Google による使用とGoogle プライバシーポリシーをご覧ください。
